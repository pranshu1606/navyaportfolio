01 · About me

Hello, I'm Navya.

I'm in my first year of the BE in Data Science and Business at Corvinus University of Budapest, a programme that sits right where numbers meet decisions. I'm drawn to the questions behind the data: why people choose what they choose, what makes a business grow, and how to explain an insight so clearly that anyone can act on it.
